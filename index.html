Enlarged perivascular spaces on VALDO 2021 Task 1: quality control of our detection chain

Twelve cases of the VALDO 2021 challenge, Task 1 (T1, T2 and FLAIR; enlarged perivascular spaces annotated by two raters). Every case is predicted by a classifier that never saw it (leave-one-case-out). Research and teaching use only: not a medical device, not for clinical decisions.

  • Our chain by verdict: green = detected, yellow = false alarm, red = missed, against rater 1. Keys: left/right case, up/down slice.
  • Chain versus its parts: classifier-kept, DRIPS-T2, MedNet-T1 and the candidate pool as switchable outlines.
  • Code and the step-by-step description of the chain: pvs-arena-chain (private during the study; ask for access). The microbleed sister project: valdo-cmb-qc.

The chain

StepWhat happens
Detector ADRIPS 3D U-Net on T2, posterior ≥ 0.10 (sensitive)
Detector BMedNet-PVS on T1, posterior ≥ 0.50 (precise)
Candidate pool26-connected components of A ∪ B
Second stage29 features per component (size, shape, membership, posteriors, z-scored intensities inside and in a 1-voxel shell, SynthSeg tissue groups); HistGradientBoosting, threshold 0.4
Metriccomponent-level F1 (a predicted component counts when it touches a reference component), bootstrap over cases

Pooled over the 12 cases against rater 1: 903 detected, 335 false alarms, 878 missed (pooled F1 0.60, mean over cases 0.57). The best single detector reaches 0.44; the ceiling set by the two raters against each other is F1 0.29 on the six cases both annotated.
